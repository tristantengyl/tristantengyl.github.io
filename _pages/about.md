## About Me

I am an incoming Postdoctoral Fellow in the **Department of Computer Science and Engineering at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)**, working with [**Prof. Mo Li**](https://home.cse.ust.hk/~lim/). I received my doctoral training in Cyberspace Security at **Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications (NJUPT)**, under the supervision of [**Prof. Fu Xiao**](https://yjs.njupt.edu.cn/dsgl/nocontrol/college/dsfcxq.htm?dsJbxxId=9B9D05C52A832DCFE050007F01006EFE) and [**Associate Prof. Pinchang Zhang**](https://mrwx481.github.io/).

My research interests lie in **Wireless Security and Cyber-Physical Systems Security**, with a particular focus on wireless physical-layer security and authentication, security in low-altitude UAV networks, and emerging security challenges in autonomous cyber-physical systems (CPS).

My long-term goal is to develop physical-evidence-driven wireless sensing, authentication, and cyber-physical security techniques to verify device identities, internal states, and physical behaviors, thereby enabling trustworthy and resilient autonomous UAV networks.

<span id="research"></span>

## Research

- **Physical-Evidence-Driven Wireless Security** — We seek stable evidence of identity and trust from RF hardware imperfections, propagation, spatial structure, and other physical-layer observations.
- **Wireless Sensing and Active Physical Verification** — We investigate whether wireless sensing and safe challenge–response mechanisms can reveal device-specific dynamics, internal states, and physical behaviors under realistic nuisance variation.
- **Trustworthy Autonomous UAV Cyber-Physical Systems** — We develop authentication, continuous trust assurance, and resilient security mechanisms for autonomous and cooperative low-altitude UAV networks.
